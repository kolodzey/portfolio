Why I created it

Open Stillness began with a very practical need: I wanted one calm place to collect the practices I kept returning to. What started as a small personal tool for organizing Yin routines gradually became something much more meaningful.

As I continued working with meditation, breathwork, and gentle movement, the project changed with me. I became less interested in simply storing practices and more interested in shaping the experience around them: creating a space that makes slowing down feel intuitive, inviting, and easy to return to.

From a small personal tool to an evolving product

Open Stillness did not begin as a platform. It started as a small personal project and gradually changed as my own practice, ideas, and ambitions for the experience grew.

2023

A personal starting point

I began with 16 hand-drawn Yin poses and a small Angular application to organize routines I was using myself.

2024

Starting again

As the idea grew, the original concept no longer felt right. I discarded the first design and application and rebuilt Open Stillness from scratch with a clearer product structure, stronger visual language, and room to expand beyond Yin practice.

Today

A seasonal product

Open Stillness now brings together Meditation, Breathwork, and Yin Stretching through seasonal experiences, supported by hand-drawn illustration, animation, original sound, and guided video.
